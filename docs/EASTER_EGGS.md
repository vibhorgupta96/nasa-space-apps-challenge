# Easter eggs (spoilers, for the team)

The game counts these as "Secrets found: x/6".

1. **The bed:** press SPACE on a bed. Mars does not let you skip the day.
2. **Earth window:** use the Earth picture on the top wall 3 times.
3. **Gerald:** the houseplant by the airlock. Use it 3 times.
4. **Bolt 42:** talk to Bolt 42 times.
5. **The tree:** from sol 5, use the seed crate to plant the "DO NOT PLANT" seed, then check the pot by the crate on a later sol.
6. **Tiny propeller:** a rover find in the north-west of the map. It unlocks the real Ingenuity/Wright Flyer fact.

Bonus: the rover's wheel tracks are stamped in the dot-dash Morse pattern for "JPL", a nod to Curiosity's real wheels (fact card in `trivia.json`).
