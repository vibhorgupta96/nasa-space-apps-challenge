# Faraway Garden demo deck

`public/demo.pptx` is the editable seven-slide submission presentation. `public/demo.pdf` is a browser-friendly image export that preserves the reviewed slide typography and three closing links. Editable text and full speaker notes remain in the PPTX. Refreshed on 15 November 2026 against the current working tree and the official [Project Submission Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/) and [Judging & Awards Guide](https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/), both retrieved that day.

## Story and coverage

1. **A child in command:** Faraway Garden, ages 7–12, Space Force, team members and the Junior Astronaut Mission Trainer challenge. The astronaut customizer makes the player the protagonist.
2. **The first morning:** crops, cooking, limited power, oxygen, dust, shelter and crew care. Notes explain ice melting and shields.
3. **Beyond the greenhouse:** route planning, auto-drive, manual 3D driving, checkpoints, battery, docking, cargo and NASA photo discoveries.
4. **The second world:** either campaign order, ten days on each world, lunar power cycles, thermal control, new crops, water-based oxygen, dust and low-gravity movement.
5. **Learning by doing:** daily sourced lessons, pet quizzes, Wiz Cards, thirteen badges, journal, rescue/retry and a saved named certificate.
6. **NASA evidence:** specific data-to-decision connections and visible boundaries between historical records, scientific facts and simulated game rules.
7. **A reason to try again:** illustrative learning goal, example certificate, current usability features, classroom pilots as a future step, and clickable game/source/team links.

The new visual theme follows the game's night-sky navy, warm cream, teal and gold, with monospaced headings and screenshots of the running local game. Screen states were staged through development controls for capture. They are not an end-to-end playthrough, proof of mission completion, or evidence of measured learning. The certificate is visibly labeled as an example. The quoted learning goal is illustrative, not a testimonial.

Only playable features are presented as current. Pre/post quiz score storage and volume settings hooks exist in code but lack player-facing controls, so the deck does not claim a pre/post quiz or volume-slider screen. System reduced-motion support and M to mute are included. A teacher guide, translations, multiplayer, APOD and scouting roadmap items are not presented as shipped.
